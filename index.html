Questions, answered

The straight version, no hype.

What is Section 21?
Section 21 of the Medicines and Related Substances Act allows a registered doctor to apply to SAHPRA for authorization to prescribe an unregistered medicine — including cannabis — for a specific patient with a qualifying condition. It is per-patient and requires records.
Who qualifies?
That decision belongs to the doctor. Patients commonly seek authorization for chronic pain, severe insomnia, anxiety, PTSD, and conditions where conventional treatment hasn't worked. Book a consultation and Dr. Tupy will assess you properly.
Can anyone just buy from you?
No — and that's the point. We only dispense to patients with a valid Section 21 authorization. We do not sell to the general public, and we don't advertise products outside the patient process. This protects you, us, and the licence.
How long does authorization take?
Timelines depend on SAHPRA processing and the completeness of the application. Our team keeps you updated on WhatsApp at every step.
Is my information private?
Yes. Patient records and authorizations are handled confidentially in line with medical records law. Courier packaging is plain and discreet.
